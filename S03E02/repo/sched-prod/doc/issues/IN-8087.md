## Question
Product asks to send reminder emails from no-reply@ instead of bookings@, because replies pile up (IN-8087). A client told all their patients to allow mail from bookings@ so reminders stay out of spam, and asks us not to change it (IN-8530). Should the sender address change?

## Options
1. Send reminders from no-reply@ for every account.
2. Keep bookings@ as the sender; handle the replies instead (auto-reply, or route them to the account's own inbox).
3. Add a per-account sender setting, with bookings@ as the default.

## Recommendation
Option 2.

## Why
Because a client told us they rely on bookings@ (IN-8530), so a change to it needs a person (AUT-2); the problem in IN-8087 is the replies, which option 2 solves without changing the address.
