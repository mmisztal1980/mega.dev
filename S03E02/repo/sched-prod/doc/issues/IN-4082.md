## Intent
Hovering over a booking in week view shows its time but not who booked it. Add the name of the person who booked to that hover card.

## Acceptance
- Given a booking by Ana Silva at 10:00, when a team member hovers it in week view, then the card shows 10:00 and Ana Silva.
- Given the hover card, when the name is added, then the time it showed is still there.
- Given the public booking page, when anyone views it, then no booker's name is shown there: names are not public.

## Boundaries
- Must not show bookers' names outside the team's week view.

Allowed by AUT-1 (new columns, filters or fields that add to what is there and take nothing away).

Request (desk): "Ticket: hovering over a booking in week view shows its time but not who booked it."
