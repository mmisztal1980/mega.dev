## Intent
The date picker on the booking page cannot be used with the keyboard alone. Make every part of it reachable and usable by keyboard.

## Acceptance
- Given the booking page, when a person tabs to the date picker, then focus lands on it and is visible.
- Given focus in the picker, when the arrow keys are pressed, then focus moves by day and week, and Enter picks the date.
- Given focus in the picker, when Escape is pressed, then it closes without changing the date and focus does not leave the page.

## Boundaries
- Must not change how dates are parsed or stored (SCHED-4471, PRD-2).
- Must not change the picker's look for mouse users.

Allowed by AUT-1 (bug fixes and small features inside the specs, with a test that shows the change).
