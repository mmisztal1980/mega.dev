## Question
Support asks to write the dates in the CSV export as dd/mm, not mm/dd, for EU accounts (IN-8541). A client's payroll import reads those dates as mm/dd today and asks us not to flip them (IN-9776). Should the export change its existing date format?

## Options
1. Change the existing date columns to dd/mm for EU accounts.
2. Keep the existing columns as they are; add a new date column in the account's locale format.
3. Keep the export as it is; SCHED-4446 adds the UTC offset, and EU clients' tools format the date.

## Recommendation
Option 2.

## Why
Because a client told us their payroll relies on mm/dd (IN-9776), so changing it needs a person (AUT-2); SCHED-4446 keeps the start and end columns as they are, and PRD-3 says an export never loses a column it had.
